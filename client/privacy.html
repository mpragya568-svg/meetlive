Privacy Policy — MeetLive

Last updated: November 10, 2026

MeetLive is a random 1-on-1 video chat website. This policy explains what information we collect and what we do with it.

Information you provide

  • Nickname and gender selection — you enter these when you set up your profile. Your nickname is shown to the stranger you are matched with.
  • Google sign-in (optional) — if you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us. We use them to show your profile and to mark you as a verified user. We do not share them with anyone else.
  • Apple sign-in (optional) — if you sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and email address with us (Apple may share a private relay email instead of your real one).

Video and audio

Your camera and microphone streams go directly between you and the person you are matched with (peer-to-peer WebRTC). MeetLive does not record, store, listen to, or watch your calls. Nothing you say or show on a call is saved on our servers.

Other information

  • Coins and gifts — your demo coin balance and gift activity are kept in the server's memory while you are online, so gifts can be delivered. They are not tied to your real identity.
  • Reports — if you report another user, the report (nicknames, reason and time) is written to a moderation log so abuse can be reviewed. Reported users can never be matched with you again.
  • Technical data — like any website, our server sees your IP address and browser type as part of operating the service.
  • Your browser — your sign-in session and nickname are stored in your own browser's local storage so you stay signed in. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser data or signing out.

What we never do

We never sell your personal information, never show you ads based on your data, and never track you across other websites.

Children

MeetLive is for adults only (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
